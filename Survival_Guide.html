Typhoon

- a giant rotating storm that brings wind, rain, and destruction. Hurricanes and Typhoons are similar cyclones. This occurs in the region of the Philippines or East and South China Seas.

If the typhoon is too strong for a heavy rain, it creates a flood that rises above the structures and grounds.

Here are the safety rules for the typhoon:

  1. Prepare an emergency kit.
  2. Evacuate
  3. Look for a higher building or a mountain, if flood occurs
  4. Stay indoors
  5. Watch the news, listen to a radio, or check the weather forecast for updates

Earthquake

-a shaking or trembling of the earth that is volcanic or tetonic in origin that caused a ground through seisemic waves in Earth's rocks.

This occurs mainly in belts that concides with tetonic plates. This creates a "Pacific Belt” which affects many regions around Pacific Ocean, along with the faults that moves within the zone.

Here are the safety rules for the earthquake:
  1. Prepare an emergency kit
  2. Evacuate
  3. Follow the duck, cover, and hold that will help you in hiding under the table, desk, etc.
  4. Never go near windows or shelves
  5. Use the emergency exit when earthquake stops and for aftershocks
  6. For outdoors, never go near the trees, posts, and buildings. Find the open area.
  7. If you're inside the car, pull over and take cover.

Photo Source | Britannica

Fire

-a rapid burning phenomenon of combustion material with the evolution of heat and usually accompanied by flame.

This occurs a destruction that happened in houses, forests, and other places which causes by accidents or when lightning strucks.

Here are the safety rules for the fire:
  1. Evacuate as soon as possible
  2. Call the fire department immediately
  3. If you have a fire extinguisher at home, use it to remove the fire.
  4. Never plug the amount of sockets. It caused fire.
  5. Watch over if it's boiling, baking etc. and turn it off when done. Leaving it can also caused fire.
  6. For the children, never play with the fire. Ask an adult for assistance
